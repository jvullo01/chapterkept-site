Privacy Policy

Chapterkept · A private reading tracker for iPhone.

The short version

Data Not Collected

Chapterkept doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. There are no third-party tools or SDKs in the app, and we run no servers for it to talk to. We never see your library.

Your library stays on this iPhone

Everything you add stays in Chapterkept on your iPhone: books, shelves, ratings, notes, reading sessions, streaks, goals, quotes and settings.

  • No sync. Chapterkept doesn't use iCloud sync or any server. If you back up your iPhone (with iCloud Backup or to a computer), Chapterkept's data is included in that backup the same way other apps' data is. We can't read it.
  • Protected on the device. The library is stored with iOS Data Protection. You can also turn on an app lock (Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode).
  • Live Activity. A running reading session can show on the Lock Screen. It's updated on your iPhone only, never by push. With the app lock on, it shows "Reading session" instead of the book title.

Book look-ups

When you scan a barcode or search for a book, your iPhone asks Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the Internet Archive) for the title, author, page count and cover. If Open Library has no match or can't be reached, it asks the Library of Congress catalog (loc.gov) instead.

  • Each request contains only the ISBN you scanned or the words you searched, plus the app's name and our support email so Open Library knows which app is asking.
  • The requests go directly from your iPhone. We never see them. No account, device ID, advertising ID or anything else about you is sent.
  • Like any website, those services receive your IP address with the request. See their own privacy policies for how they handle it.
  • Look-ups happen only when you start one. Turn on Offline mode in Privacy and Chapterkept never connects; you can add books by typing them in.

Read to me and quotes happen on your iPhone

Read to me uses your iPhone's camera, Apple's on-device text recognition and on-device speech. Page photos and the recognized text stay in memory and are removed when you close the reader. Nothing is saved to Photos.

Quotes use Live Text and on-device text recognition. The page photo is kept with a quote only if you turn on "Keep the photo" (off by default); a kept photo has its location and camera details removed. Quote cards are made on your iPhone and leave it only if you share them.

On-device AI

Pro features that use Apple Intelligence, such as grouping your quotes by theme, run on Apple's on-device model on supported iPhones. They run entirely on your iPhone and never use a server, including Apple's Private Cloud Compute. If on-device AI isn't available, those features are hidden and everything else works the same.

Import, export and backups you make

You can import a CSV file or a Chapterkept backup that you pick, and export a CSV or a backup file to wherever you choose. Files are read or written only when you ask. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. We never receive it.

Purchases

Chapterkept Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, email, card or Apple Account details.

Deleting your data

Privacy > Erase all Chapterkept data deletes everything Chapterkept stored on your iPhone. This can't be undone. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Chapterkept collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Chapterkept ever starts collecting anything, this policy and the App Store privacy label will say so first.
