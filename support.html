Support

Chapterkept · A private reading tracker for iPhone.

Contact

Email jvappsupport+chapterkept@proton.me. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. There's no need to send your library. We usually reply within a few days.

Moving to a new iPhone

Chapterkept doesn't sync. If you restore your new iPhone from a backup of the old one, your library comes with it. You can also open Library > Import & export on the old iPhone, export a backup file, and import it on the new one.

Bringing books from another app

Export a CSV file from the other app, then open Library > Import & export and tap Choose a CSV file. Chapterkept detects the columns, shows a preview, and lets you undo the import.

A book isn't found when I scan or search

Look-ups use Open Library, then the Library of Congress. Some books aren't in either catalog. Tap Enter manually to type the title, author and page count. Offline mode also turns look-ups off.

Read to me or Live Text doesn't pick up a page

Hold the page flat in good light and fill the frame. Everything is recognized on your iPhone, so it works offline. For quotes, you can always type the words instead.

The AI features don't appear

They need iOS 26 or later and an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Chapterkept uses only the on-device model, so on other iPhones these features are hidden and everything else works the same.

Restoring Pro or sharing it with family

Pro is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing. On a new iPhone or for a family member, open Privacy and tap Restore Purchase.

Erasing everything

Privacy > Erase all Chapterkept data removes everything Chapterkept stored on your iPhone. This can't be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it later.

Privacy

Chapterkept collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
